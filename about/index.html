About

I'm Saurabh Mehta, a hands-on product builder with 15+ years across B2B and B2C. I spend most of my time on the adoption and enhancement layer of AI, closing the gap between what agents can do and what people want them to do.

Far & Close is where I write down what I learn by building, along with the occasional musing on where AI is headed.

Things I've built

  • Markwise. Google-Docs-style comments and suggested edits for the markdown your agent writes, saved inside the file so any agent can act on them. Feedback to agents shouldn't get lost in chat.
  • AI Agent FM. Turns your coding-agent session traces into a private podcast episode about how you built something. The real story of a build is in the traces, and nobody reads them.
  • PM Workspace. A self-organising second brain for product managers. PMs at my current workplace now use it, and it saves each of them about two hours a day.
